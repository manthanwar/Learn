# Dell Inspiron 5579 (2-in-1) Laptop

## Ports

The Dell Inspiron 15 5579 (2-in-1) features a traditional selection of connection ports including two USB 3.1 Gen 1 ports, one USB 2.0 port, and an HDMI 1.4b port. For full hardware insights, check the official Dell Inspiron 5579 Setup and Specifications guide.

### Left Side Ports

* Power adapter port: Connects the barrel-type AC power supply
* HDMI 1.4b port: Outputs video and audio to external monitors or TVs
* USB 3.1 Gen 1 with PowerShare: Connects high-speed peripherals and charges devices while the laptop sleeps
* USB 3.1 Gen 1: Connects standard high-speed USB devices

### Right Side Ports

* SD card reader: Reads full-size SD, SDHC, and SDXC cards
* USB 2.0 port: Connects legacy mice, keyboards, or flash drives
* Headset port: Combines headphone and microphone jack (3.5mm)
* Noble wedge lock slot: Secures the laptop physically with a compatible lock
